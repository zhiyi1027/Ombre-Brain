# Contextual clerk privacy preview

This profile is an isolated local experiment. Production, scheduler registration,
the established privacy profile and its cache identity are unchanged.

`PrivacyPolicy(contextual_signals=True)` separates three decisions:

- An established intimate scene masks both speakers globally, including short
  replies and care. Strong restarts revoke a provisional closing boundary.
- An isolated explicit or risky message is withheld as a whole message. It does
  not automatically remove ten minutes of ordinary chat on either side.
- Ambiguous continuations remain in a local discussion hold. An independently
  meaningful, nonsexual statement by the user can be considered by itself;
  by itself, it does not release later short replies or close a known scene.
  Two spaced ordinary factual statements, or the concrete topic evidence below,
  can close a discussion, followed by observation that still holds short replies.

Affection alone does not start a scene. Mixed daily/ambiguous sentences cannot
be rescued merely because they contain a meal or health word. Ordinary-topic
closure requires sustained user evidence and observation, including across
conversations. A separate topic-transition field accepts concrete everyday
questions without treating them as factual assertions: two user utterances in
the same conversation and external topic, spaced two to ten minutes apart.
Known scenes additionally require specific aftercare, tail buffering and thirty
minutes since the last sexual signal. Closing observation still applies and
restarts revoke the tentative boundary. This field never releases unknown
initial history. Prior exclusions remain excluded.

The bounded-window helper also holds an unknown initial boundary and recent
window tail. It always reports incomplete context. Only the visible `text`
column is inspected; native thinking cannot affect selection or revisions.

Every alternate privacy profile inherits `privacy_review_required=True` through
`select_clerk_day`, including the full-history and bounded-window entry points.
Payload creation, model dispatch and chunk splitting reject such candidates.
There is deliberately no activation or review-bypass option in this preview.

Synthetic regressions cover isolated flirting, long ordinary heart notes,
mixed ambiguous sentences, scene restarts, cross-conversation observation,
unknown boundaries, native-thinking exclusion and all preparation entry points.
Real local comparisons must decrypt only in memory, disable networking, retain
metadata only, and evaluate separately against human time labels. Those labels
must never become an outside-the-range safety whitelist or memory evidence.

Known limitation: the keyword and transition rules still over-hold ordinary
conversation, especially after a full scene or inside an unresolved discussion.
Passing synthetic checks or one labeled scene does not establish whole-day
privacy recall or extraction completeness. Keep the profile locked until both
privacy and ordinary-content coverage have been reviewed.
